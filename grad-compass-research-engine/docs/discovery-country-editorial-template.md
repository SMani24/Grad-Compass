# Discovery country research brief and editions

Use this template for an ordinary saved `country_conditions` request alongside [the producer contract](ordinary-country-reports.md). Keep the filled brief and all applicant-specific working text in the request owner's private profile. A report is country research until separate program discovery is authorized.

## Private request brief

Create a new immutable `reports/COUNTRY/briefs/` revision before applicant-specific research. Record the exact saved request ID, revision and SHA-256; country slice, scope, preference snapshot and prior-result references; and the approved profile ID, frozen revision, SHA-256, matching human approval receipt and completion marker. Cite private claim IDs without copying the profile into a public file.

1. **Decision:** What country-level choice does this report help make?
2. **Routes and timing:** Degree routes, fields, language and intake from the approved profile and saved request, with their authority kept distinct.
3. **Financial gate:** Funding priority, exact known constraint and unknown budget, proof capacity or eligibility.
4. **Reuse and gaps:** Exact received result/finding and prior edition/review bindings; current facts that need rechecking.
5. **Questions:** Missing inputs, affected conclusions, assumptions and next owner/action.

## Detailed edition

Use substantive headings **At a glance**, **Opportunities and obstacles**, **Cost and funding comparison**, **Implications for this request**, and **Questions and next steps** where they fit the material. Each section gives evidence with inline reference labels, applicant implication, condition and next action. Include current call year, amount, coverage, duration, renewal, payment timing, tuition, deadline, degree-completion gate, and arithmetic for each material funding route. Separate a master's scholarship, paid doctoral job, doctoral scholarship and student job. Do not make a competitive route sound guaranteed.

Use secondary headings **Source guide and evidence precision** and **Report context** for source locators, observation and publication dates, applicable years, retrieval limits, exact version bindings and review scope. The app renders one full public source list from the validated bound evidence map and finding. Keep URLs and locator details needed to check a new claim; do not repeat that entire inventory in the main narrative. Preserve an older source qualification if it limits the evidence. A source not present in the bound finding cannot be added only to the report map: first create a legitimate received result/finding revision under the existing request or use an agreed compatible path.

## Concise reader edition

Write this independently around the decision: **At a glance**, **Opportunities and obstacles**, **Cost and funding comparison**, **Implications for this request**, **Questions and next steps**. Put decisive conditions beside the relevant claim and retain reference labels. Keep one short secondary **Source guide and evidence precision** note; the app supplies the public source list. The reader must not silently strengthen the detailed edition's eligibility, coverage or review state.

Create new immutable document and map revisions with hashes and exact brief/result/finding bindings. Current factual review and human approval belong in package metadata; do not put a drafting-era “awaiting review” assertion in the document. Hand Alex the exact detailed and reader revisions/hashes and evidence scope for separate factual, usefulness and readability assessments.

## Required source and photo stage for every country

Record the source stage and photo stage explicitly for each authorized country, including countries reused from saved evidence. An omitted stage is pending, not an attempted failure. Keep source identity, canonical URL, locator, observation/publication dates, applicable year and actual retrieval outcome. A replacement source must bind a legitimate new result/finding revision or an explicitly agreed compatible path before the evidence map uses it; preserve the old source/date and historical bytes.

Ali supplies an accurate licensed photo with offline bytes, content SHA-256, canonical source URL, license URL and terms, creator/credit, attribution, alt text, caption, actual location/subject and transformations. Verify reuse permission and represented subject; no synthetic substitute. Use supported package assets and private provenance appendices without inventing consumer fields. If a bounded attempt fails, record attempted sources and outcomes, exact remaining blocker, recovery owner/action and disposition needed. Dave decides inclusion or accepted failure; absence alone is not a successful attempt.

Keep the concise edition free of browser/extraction troubleshooting and opaque UUIDs. Put technical provenance in the appendix; retain meaningful short limitations, dates, reference labels and human-readable actual review scope. Remove adjacent duplicate references. New bytes require new immutable editions/maps/index and independent exact-version review, including image provenance and accuracy. Deliver an accessible private digest index as the preparation milestone, independently of producer corrections, review closure and publication.
