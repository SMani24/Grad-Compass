# Ordinary country report execution contract

This applies to every real `country_conditions` request exported from the app, not only the current Germany and Canada revisions. Dave routes the saved request to Discovery Researcher, then Alex reviews exact editions independently. The application never invokes an agent. The saved request/response 0.1.0, report notice 0.1, private package 0.2 and public exchange 1.0.0 contracts remain unchanged.

## 1. Bound private brief before research

Read the exact saved request and `.ref.json` from the request owner's private profile. Verify the request SHA-256 and its country, scope, limits, preference snapshot and prior-result references. Resolve the exact approved Applicant Profile from its established access-controlled store; verify its frozen revision, SHA-256, matching human approval receipt and completion marker before using applicant claims. Record opaque profile ID, revision, digest and approval receipt ID in an owner-private brief. Do not copy the profile or intake into a comment, Git file, public batch or report export. If approval or access cannot be verified, keep general country research useful but mark applicant-specific conclusions pending and have Dave resolve the exact gap.

Create a new immutable brief revision with request ID/revision/digest, country and scope, profile ID/revision/digest, preparation time, owner and these compact fields:

1. Decision this report helps the applicant make.
2. Relevant degree routes, fields, language and intake from approved claims and the request preference snapshot; cite their private claim IDs without reproducing source text.
3. Financial constraints, funding priorities and eligibility questions. Do not turn an unknown budget, degree completion date or citizenship condition into a default.
4. Reusable findings with exact result/finding revision and review state; gaps that require current investigation.
5. Missing inputs, assumptions, affected conclusions and next action.

The request preference snapshot is the request's authority for request-time preferences. A later profile or preference change requires a new brief revision and explicit reconciliation; it never silently changes the saved request. Separate country facts, applicant implications and unverified eligibility. Brief revision/hash belongs in the private producer handoff and task acceptance record, not the public response schema.

## 2. Research and editions

Discovery Researcher owns research and substantive editorial text. For each substantive topic, state the evidence, implication for this request, conditions or uncertainty, and next action. Produce a complete detailed Markdown edition and independently written concise reader edition, both tied to the same brief, result/finding and evidence map. Keep the concise narrative focused on opportunities, obstacles, costs/funding and practical steps, with conditions beside claims. Keep the detailed edition useful before university discovery, with precise source sections/locators, calculations and unresolved questions. Never use a schema field count or source count as a proxy for usefulness.

Use existing evidence-map reference labels inline with factual claims. The app renders one public source list from the validated, bound evidence map and received finding records; neither narrative edition should repeat a full source inventory. Discovery still supplies source identities, exact URLs, locators, dates, evidence precision and coverage in the map. Keep **At a glance**, **Opportunities and obstacles**, **Cost and funding comparison**, **Implications for this request**, and **Questions and next steps** substantive. Place explicit **Coverage** or **Seven-topic coverage**, **Sources** or **Source guide and evidence precision**, and **Report context** or **Technical provenance** in secondary sections. Unknown headings remain substantive. The app may present validated coverage within Report details; it must not synthesize missing applicant content. Keep legacy narrative qualifications accessible as historical context, without presenting them as current review receipts.

For material funding, costs, deadlines and residence claims, prefer primary sources. Record observation date, source publication/update date when available, applicable academic year, and retrieval outcome separately. Preserve historical figures with their actual year; a retrieval failure is not a confirmed broken link. Check scheme amount, coverage, duration, renewal, payment timing, tuition treatment, eligibility and deadlines, including whether bachelor completion is required at deadline or enrolment. Distinguish master's scholarships, paid doctoral jobs, doctoral scholarships and student work, including academic entry requirements. Explain what was checked when a route remains unresolved. Show arithmetic and avoid counting insurance twice. Programme-dependent questions may stay open, with their effect stated.

An evidence map must keep source IDs, exact URLs/locators, dates, applicable year, topic/claim precision and unresolved scope. Its source records must match the exact bound received finding. New official-source research requires a legitimate result/finding revision through the existing saved request, or an agreed compatible path, before the map can bind it; do not append new source records to a map bound to an older finding. Finding-level evidence or unreviewed editorial topic mapping stays labelled at that precision. A revised fact, source, brief, detailed edition or reader edition creates a new immutable revision; historical artifacts and legitimate evidence-scope qualifications remain available.

## 3. Independent review and mutable state

Use the [independent review checklist](independent-country-review.md) for exact-byte checks, three-dimension dispositions, metadata reconciliation and correction handoffs.

Alex assesses three dimensions separately for each exact detailed and reader revision/hash: **factual accuracy and evidence support**, **useful coverage and request relevance**, and **readability and consistency between editions**. Each dimension records pass, changes required or unresolved, concrete omissions/corrections, reviewer, UTC time and exact edition binding. A factual pass does not imply useful coverage or readable editions. Discovery revises content on changes required and returns new hashes for review.

The authoritative package metadata holds the current canonical and reader factual review states and their exact document revision/hash bindings. The task review record can add the three-dimension assessment; it does not override package metadata. A reviewer disposition is never inferred from a prior research finding, a report receipt, or an older edition. Human approval remains separate and unknown/not requested until an authorized exact-version record exists. Correct stale current “awaiting review” wording through a new content or metadata revision after the actual disposition; do not rewrite historical files or erase valid evidence-scope cautions.

## 4. Private delivery and acceptance

Use the existing owner-private saved request profile, inbox and package publisher described in [local-response-inbox.md](local-response-inbox.md) and [country-report-contract.md](../../grad-compass-workspace/design/country-report-contract.md). Publish acknowledgement and partial/final response progress independently. After review and release disposition, run the package publisher `--check`, then publish the finalized package; the notice is emitted last. A response marked complete means its bounded research response is complete, not that every topic is resolved or a report is reviewed.

Before closing an ordinary execution task, record: verified request/profile/brief identities; exact result/finding and both edition/map/package revisions and hashes; source and seven-topic coverage checks; Alex's three separate exact-edition assessments; package metadata review and human state readback; publisher receipt plus app normal-path receipt/replay; prior-artifact preservation; and the next owner/action for any unresolved item. Use synthetic data for public examples and tests. Germany/Canada publication is a separate dependent delivery task, not evidence that this reusable path has run for a future applicant.
