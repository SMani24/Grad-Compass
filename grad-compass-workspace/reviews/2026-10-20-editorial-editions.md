# Received country editions

The normal app now consumes the delivered Belgium concise and detailed editorial sidecars. Both bind the existing canonical report and evidence map; their actual factual review remains pending and human approval remains unknown. The app does not infer acceptance from availability or an earlier source-artifact review. Producer files and canonical metadata are unchanged.

**Overview** opens the received concise text. **Read full report** opens the delivered detailed research, with document-specific navigation. Inline E references open a keyboard-accessible source dialog rather than repeating evidence rows after every passage. The source section retains expandable details, and public URLs and evidence qualifications remain accessible. Header spacing is tighter, with shared origin and export purpose visible. Existing sidebar, Comfortable/Wide widths, annotation-space recovery and subtle map resize controls remain.

**Download PDF** produces the intentionally shorter reader rendition. **Export detailed Markdown** contains the complete detailed edition and evidence map, all dated public source records and passage references. Both export links pin the selected canonical metadata edition and exact editorial IDs. Choosing an earlier report changes displayed content and both exports; private annotation inclusion resets off. Response revisions, canonical versions, editorial revisions and PDF layout identifiers remain distinct.

The print layout separates the flag from branding, preserves the photograph's subject in a modest credited inset, repeats table headers and uses compact linked references and a two-column source guide. Tables can continue after their first row rather than forcing a largely empty cover. Exact origin hashes remain in PDF properties and the detailed Markdown, avoiding a trailing technical-only page. The reader PDF contains no internal agent/workflow instructions.

Rewritten editorial annotations bind their own exact retained document and passage. Earlier canonical and PDF selectors are unchanged. Old highlights never appear automatically on rewritten passages or newly laid-out PDF pages. Returning to an annotation reopens its original document/PDF. Reading positions are similarly separated by canonical/editorial identity, and ordinary backup/recovery includes the new private editorial cache.
